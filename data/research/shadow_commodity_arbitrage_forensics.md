### 1. The Russian Seaborne "Dark Fleet" Transshipment Architecture

To evade the G7/EU maritime services ban—which prohibits Western shipowners, maritime insurers, and flag registries from facilitating Russian oil shipments unless sold below the $60/bbl price cap—Russia and its intermediaries assembled a self-contained shadow fleet.

- **Insurance Evasion:** Completely detached from the International Group of P&I Clubs (e.g., Gard, UK P&I, Britannia). Insured through state-backed Russian insurer **Ingosstrakh** or obscure domestic underwriters lacking international reinsurance capacity.


- Between 2023 and 2026, the **Gabon maritime registry** expanded its registered tanker tonnage by over **600%**, becoming the primary registry of choice for Sovcomflot-linked vessels.

#### Ship-to-Ship (STS) Choke Points
To obscure cargo provenance and optimize logistics, dark fleet tankers engage in mid-ocean STS transfers, transferring crude from smaller ice-class Aframax vessels (loading in Baltic ports) to Very Large Crude Carriers (VLCCs) bound for Asia:

3. **Port Said / Suez Anchorage (Egypt):** Northern and southern approaches to the Suez Canal.
4. **Fujairah Anchorage (Gulf of Oman):** Key regional bunkering and oil blending hub.

#### AIS Spoofing Telemetry

- **GPS Coordinates Spoofing:** Vessels transmit static positions at anchor in international waters while satellite synthetic aperture radar (SAR) imagery reveals them loading at Ust-Luga or Primorsk.

#### The "Substantial Transformation" Legal Mechanism
Under global customs jurisprudence formalized by the **World Customs Organization (WCO)** and codified in **EU Customs Code (Regulation (EU) No 952/2013, Article 60)** and **19 CFR § 134.1 (US Customs)**:
- Goods undergo origin alteration if they experience a "substantial transformation" resulting in a new and different article of commerce having a new name, character, and use.
- In oil refining, unrefined crude petroleum (**HS Code 2709**) is cracked, desulfurized, and distilled into ultra-low sulfur diesel, jet fuel, or gasoline (**HS Code 2710**).
- This tariff shift legally strips the product of its Russian origin. The refined fuel is legally certified as **100% Indian Origin** and imported into European ports (Rotterdam, Antwerp) and the United States without violating sanctions.
